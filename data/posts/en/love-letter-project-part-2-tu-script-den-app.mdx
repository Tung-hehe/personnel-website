In [part one](/en/blog/love-letter-project), Love Letter was built to do one thing: write love letters.
The 11 templates back then all revolved around the same theme - romance. But the more I worked on it,
the more I realized what I actually loved wasn't "writing love letters" - it was sitting down and turning
hard-to-say things into words, and that isn't something romance has a monopoly on. So Love Letter slowly
grew past its own name <Twemoji emoji="sparkles" />.

## No longer just about two people

From 11 templates built purely around romance, the project now has 19: friendship, siblings, "comrades,"
family (in 3-, 4- and 5-person versions), farewell letters, and celebration letters.

## Turning it into a web app

At first, this project was just for me. But as the number of templates kept growing, I started wanting
to share it with more people - and the CLI version, handy as it was for a programmer, wasn't exactly
something you hand to your family or friends to try. So I built a web app with Astro + TypeScript,
deployed at [love-letter.tungtt.dev](https://love-letter.tungtt.dev/): open the link and start writing
right away, nothing to install.

This was the first time I'd built a full web app, and my first time touching Astro too. Lucky for me, AI
help exists now (I used Claude), so learning and building at the same time wasn't nearly as rough as it
used to be - the whole thing came together in under a week. Building a product while picking up a new
piece of tech on the side felt worth it.

If you can, try the app on desktop to get the full experience of what I built.

The CLI is still around for anyone who wants to dig deeper, along with a new script -
`generate_all.py` - that renders the same content against all 19 templates at once, handy for comparing
which one fits best.

## Closing thoughts

I honestly didn't expect a small spark of inspiration to grow this far. There's a bit of bittersweetness
to it too - the original goal, writing a letter for one specific person, isn't the only thing this
project is about anymore. But that's fine; getting to share it with more people is its own kind of joy.

If you try it and run into a bug, or have an idea for a new letter theme, leave a comment - I'll keep
building on it.
